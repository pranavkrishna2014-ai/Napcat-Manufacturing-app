Foam Block Cutting

Each cutting order holds its block details (grade, size, supplier, invoice). Length & breadth in inches, height & sheet thickness in mm. Record the actual block size and sheets cut — the contingent (block CFT − sheets CFT) is calculated automatically and fresh sheets flow into that foam's stock.
